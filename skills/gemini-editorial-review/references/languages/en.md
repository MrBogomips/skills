# English (en)

Notes added to the system instruction when the text is in English. The `Stopwords:` line is used by the script
to detect the language (lowercase, space separated).

Stopwords: the and of to is in that it for with as on are this be by not or from but have which you can will

## Language notes: English

- Keep one spelling convention (American or British) throughout, the one the source uses most.
- Cut the stock vocabulary of machine-written English: *delve*, *leverage*, *robust*, *seamless*, *crucial*,
  *pivotal*, *landscape*, *realm*, *tapestry*, *navigate* (for anything but navigation), *unlock*, *empower*,
  *elevate*, *game-changer*, *in today's fast-paced world*.
- Prefer plain verbs to nominalizations (*decide*, not *make a decision*; *use*, not *utilize*).
- Dashes: an occasional em dash is natural; one in every paragraph is a tell. Prefer commas, parentheses,
  colons or a new sentence.
- Headings: keep the case style the source uses consistently; do not switch to Title Case midway.
- Contractions follow the profile: natural in `blog`, `marketing` and `creative`, sparing in `technical` and
  `professional`.
